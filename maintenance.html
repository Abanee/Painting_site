Property Maintenance

Every property has
a maintenance rhythm.

01
Monthly

Quick visual inspection for visible stains, peeling, moisture marks or unusual cracks.

02
Seasonal

Inspect after heavy weather — exterior walls, terrace areas, balconies and exposed surfaces.

03
Annual

Detailed property review — paint condition, surface defects, dampness and vulnerable areas.

04
Long-Term

Plan repainting and major maintenance based on the property's actual condition.

Actual maintenance intervals depend on surface condition, environment, exposure and materials — the rhythm above is a general guide, not a fixed schedule.

Professional Inspection

Some things are
better checked properly.

A visual check can help you notice changes, but persistent moisture, recurring cracks or widespread coating failure may require professional assessment.

01

Observe

Notice visible changes — colour, texture or surface behaviour that's different from before.

02

Record

Photograph the area and note where the issue appears, so any change over time is easy to track.

03

Assess

Get professional guidance when the issue persists, spreads or worsens over successive checks.

If something you've noticed keeps recurring, it's worth having it looked at properly.
