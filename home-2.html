01 Peeling Paint

Improper surface preparation or old coating failure. Needs surface prep + primer + finish.

02 Wall Cracks

Settlement or plaster movement. Needs crack filling + reinforcement + flexible coating.

03 Dampness

Moisture trapped under old layers. Needs source detection + damp-proof treatment.

04 Water Seepage

Weak waterproofing membrane. Needs membrane repair + waterproofing system.

05 Faded Exterior

UV exposure over time. Needs surface cleaning + UV-resistant top coat.
