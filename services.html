Start with the problem

What's happening to your surface?

Every visible failure has an underlying root cause. Identify your property condition below to see our engineered multi-step treatment protocol.

Problem 01

Wall Cracks & Fissures

Cause: Thermal Settlement

Micro-cracks widen over seasons, allowing rainwater to penetrate masonry and corrode internal reinforcement.

Engineered Solution Pipeline:
1V-Groove Polymer Stitching
→
2Alkali-Resistant Primer
→
3Elastomeric Coating
Problem 02

Dampness & Efflorescence

Cause: Capillary Moisture

Trapped dampness causes white salt deposits, destroys plaster adhesion, and leads to unhealthy indoor mold.

Engineered Solution Pipeline:
1Infrared Moisture Audit
→
2Hydrophobic Membrane
→
3Anti-Damp Breathable Top
Problem 03

Peeling & Flaking Paint

Cause: Adhesion Failure

Poor previous surface preparation or low-grade primer causes topcoats to blister, peel, and detach in sheets.

Engineered Solution Pipeline:
1Surface Scraping & Wash
→
2Deep Penetrating Primer
→
3High-Bond Velvet Finish
Problem 04

Faded & Chalking Facade

Cause: UV & Weather Degradation

Prolonged sun exposure breaks down paint binders, causing chalky residue, color loss, and reduced weather resistance.

Engineered Solution Pipeline:
1High-Pressure Bio-Wash
→
2Elastic Substrate Sealer
→
3100% WeatherShield Silicone
Service scope

What happens before the final coat?

01
Site Condition Review

We inspect the surface, note existing damage and identify the root cause of any problem before quoting a solution.

02
Surface Protection

Floors, fixtures and furniture are masked and covered so the surrounding property stays clean throughout the work.

03
Cleaning & Repair

Cracks, loose material and old coatings are removed and repaired so the new system bonds properly.

04
Primer / Base System

The right primer or base coat is applied for the surface type, improving adhesion and the longevity of the finish.

05
Finish Application

Our trained applicators apply each coat with consistent technique, timing and coverage.

06
Final Cleanup

Masking is removed, surfaces are wiped down and the site is left as clean as we found it.

07
Quality Check

We walk through the finished surface together and inspect every detail before handover.
